A Kingdom Written in Blood

A Royal Tragedy of Love, Betrayal and Destiny
WRITTEN BY MAYANJA SHAKIB · 12 ACTS

Within the ancient kingdom of Inkarnia, the struggle for power draws love, loyalty and ambition into a dangerous contest. A royal drama of political intrigue, supernatural forces, war and the choices that shape a kingdom.

Reading edition in preparation. The acts and scenes will be added from the author's approved manuscript. No scenes have been invented or substituted on this page.
